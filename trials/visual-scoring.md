# Scoring visual fidelity
Freeze the reference, viewing scale, representative ratios and scoring anchors before dispatch. Blind the reviewer to the run conditions. Score overall fidelity first, at intended viewing size; do not calculate it by averaging details. Separately rate shape, size/proportion, modeled surface detail and visible color/finish. Cite visible evidence per score.
Use 1 for wrong or absent, 2 for generic approximation, 3 for recognizable with a defining mismatch, 4 for close with minor differences, and 5 for faithful to visible intent. Mark hidden properties not assessable and unrequested treatments not applicable. Do not reward added complexity for its own sake. Separate camera or lighting uncertainty from geometry, and technical pass/fail checks from visual quality.

Orientation: name the feature and what it should face relative to the part, not the camera. Score 1 for a wrong defining axis or reversed facing, 2 for a major angular error, 3 for correct general direction with a conspicuous tilt, 4 for minor deviation, 5 for agreement within what the reference resolves. Use not assessable if the reference hides the direction, or not applicable if rotation makes no visible difference.
