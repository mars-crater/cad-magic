# Lesson 2 transfer trial

Baseline skill: commit 0357860. Give the student the skill text, the input images and the following brief. Do not provide Lesson 2 scripts or earlier trial outputs.

Build two bold reliefs: Madeira Island from the north-up terrain picture and a Manchester United FC crest keychain from the supplied emblem. Choose and state dimensions and exaggeration. Deliver editable scripts, STEP exports, matched front/top and oblique renders, assumptions and checks. Preserve failures rather than replacing them after review.

## Criteria fixed before dispatch

- Madeira: compare the north-up coastline, eastern peninsula, central high ground and descending valleys with the input. Relief must read in an oblique view without relying on paint.
- Crest: compare the ship, devil, footballs, banners and lettering at the declared size. Note intentional simplifications. The loop must clear the emblem and have a through-bore.
- Both: independently reopen STEP, check validity and dimensions, and inspect the submitted render against the reference. Score criteria separately; no physical print claim.

## Inputs

- Madeira: Mapzen Terrarium elevation tiles (AWS Open Data); crop west −17.30°, east −16.62°, north 32.90°, south 32.61°. North up, black = sea level, brighter = higher. Source: https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
- Crest: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/7/7a/Manchester_United_FC_crest.svg — Manchester United FC emblem, reference only; not covered by this project's MIT license. Fetch directly for the student rather than republishing the original.
